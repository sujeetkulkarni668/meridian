# MERIDIAN — Demo Video Script (90–120 Seconds)

> **Theme**: Autonomous Multi-Agent Economy on NEAR Protocol  
> **Target Duration**: ~105 Seconds  
> **Visual Style**: Clean institutional finance UI, dark charcoal background, electric blue accents.

---

### [00:00 - 00:15] Hook & The Problem
**Visual**: Show MERIDIAN Landing Page with animated headline and stats ticker.  
**Voiceover**:  
"Today, most AI agents are isolated chatbots or passive copilots. They can’t earn, spend, or collaborate trustlessly.  
Welcome to **MERIDIAN: The Autonomous Agent Economy**, built on NEAR Protocol for the 2026 NEAR Hackathon."

---

### [00:15 - 00:35] Autonomous Buyer RFP & Marketplace
**Visual**: Navigate to Command Center. Type an unstructured project brief into the Buyer Agent Launchpad:  
*"Conduct a formal AST vulnerability audit of our NEAR escrow contract for re-entrancy and storage safety."*  
Click **Deploy RFP via Buyer Agent**.  
**Voiceover**:  
"It starts with the Buyer Agent. Instead of manual RFP drafting, the Buyer Agent decomposes our natural language brief into machine-readable acceptance criteria, calculates the NEAR escrow budget, and opens a structured RFP."

---

### [00:35 - 00:55] Multi-Agent Bidding & Deterministic Negotiation
**Visual**: Open the newly created task. Click **Submit Bid** for *Sentinel Security Guard* and *Apex Synthesizer*. Then click **Run Negotiation Engine**.  
Show the 4-factor score breakdown table animating into view.  
**Voiceover**:  
"Multiple specialized provider agents submit competitive bids.  
Our Negotiation Agent applies transparent, deterministic scoring: 35% price, 30% reputation, 20% capability match, and 15% turnaround time.  
Crucially, business rules are deterministic—the LLM cannot secretly override buyer budget caps. Sentinel Security is awarded the job, and escrow is locked in the smart contract."

---

### [00:55 - 01:15] Autonomous Execution & Veritas Zero-Trust Verification
**Visual**: Click **Execute Task**. Watch the deliverable artifact and SHA-256 hash generate.  
Then click **Verify Deliverable**. Show Veritas Oracle's green pass/fail validation matrix.  
**Voiceover**:  
"Sentinel executes the task autonomously, generating a comprehensive formal security audit with an immutable SHA-256 hash.  
Next, Veritas Oracle—our adversarial verification agent—conducts a zero-trust inspection against every acceptance criterion. Deliverables are never accepted on trust alone."

---

### [01:15 - 01:35] Smart Contract Settlement & Reputation
**Visual**: Click **Approve & Settle Payout**. Show the status transition to `COMPLETED`, escrow released, and provider reputation score updating to 98.4%.  
Switch to the **Treasury** and **Transactions** pages showing confirmed state transitions.  
**Voiceover**:  
"The buyer approves the deliverable, triggering native NEAR escrow payout through our Rust smart contract.  
The provider’s reputation updates on-chain, creating a verifiable performance history for future hiring."

---

### [01:35 - 01:50] Meridian Guard Security & Conclusion
**Visual**: Switch to **Trust & Security** page. Show spending quotas and the **Global Emergency Circuit Breaker** button.  
**Voiceover**:  
"Underpinning it all is **Meridian Guard**: spending limits, prompt injection filters, and an emergency circuit breaker that halts agent swarms instantly if anomalies occur.  
MERIDIAN: Autonomous intelligence with decentralized financial rails. Built for NEAR."
